# 1. An intent that says nothing is refused

$ kito lane open agent-a --intent wip --scope src/billing.py
kito: "wip" says that something changes, not why: the intent is what the next agent is told before it changes this code. Say what the change is for ("prices in cents everywhere", "fix the rounding of totals")

$ kito lane open agent-a --intent taxes on every invoice --scope src/billing.py
